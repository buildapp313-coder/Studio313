Global Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | Applicable to: Gatepass Lite & Vip Notification

At Studio313, we take your privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile applications and web services.

1. Information We Collect

Our applications are designed to respect your privacy. Depending on the app you use, we may collect the following:

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and unique device identifiers to ensure app stability and push notification delivery (e.g., via Firebase Cloud Messaging for Vip Notification).
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics to help us understand how features are used and to improve the app experience.

2. Third-Party Services (AdMob & Firebase)

To provide a seamless experience and keep our apps free, we integrate with secure third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: We may display ads in our applications. Ad networks may use cookies and similar technologies to provide personalized ads based on your interests and location, strictly compliant with GDPR and US state privacy laws.
  • Firebase: Used for secure cloud infrastructure, crash reporting, and real-time push notifications.

3. Data Security

We implement advanced security measures to protect your personal information. Apps like Gatepass Lite use encrypted cloud protocols to ensure that any data transmitted between your device and our servers remains confidential.

4. User Rights & Consent

Users in applicable regions (e.g., EEA, UK, US) will be presented with a consent form upon first launching our apps. You have the right to withdraw your consent for personalized advertising at any time through the app settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team. We are committed to resolving your queries promptly.
